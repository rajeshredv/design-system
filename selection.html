Checkbox & radio

Controls for picking options. A checkbox turns one option on or off, or selects several. A radio picks exactly one option from a group. The check circle is a round checkbox for selecting items in a list.

Figma: Check-box · Radio · multiselect Nodes 2050:3150 · 2050:3169 · 2050:3106 13 variants Light & dark

All three are native <input> elements with appearance: none. The Figma variants come from the input's own :checked, :indeterminate and :disabled states, so there are no state classes to manage. Looking for the switch? See Toggle.

Accessibility

  • It's a real <input type="checkbox">: Space toggles it and screen readers announce "checked", "not checked" or "mixed" (Semi).
  • Give every checkbox a name: a wrapping <label> or aria-label. The 24px input is the hit target, and the whole label is clickable.
  • Focus shows a 2px --color-button-1000 ring 2px outside the box. Figma has no focus state, so it was added in code.
  • Contrast: the Uncheck ring (Text/T2) is about 2.1:1 on white in light mode and 2.9:1 on #2B2B2B in dark mode, below the WCAG 3:1 minimum for controls. Disabled states are only shown by 50% opacity.

Accessibility

  • Give every radio in a group the same name. The browser then makes the group one tab stop and arrow keys move the selection.
  • Label the group: role="radiogroup" with aria-label, or a <fieldset> with a <legend>. Label each option with a wrapping <label>.
  • Focus shows a 2px --color-button-1000 ring 2px outside the 20px frame (code only).
  • Contrast: the Active ring has the same low contrast as the checkbox ring (about 2.1:1 light, 2.9:1 dark).

Accessibility

  • It's a native checkbox, so it's announced as a checkbox with checked, not checked or mixed states.
  • It has no visible text, so always pass an aria-label that names the item, like "Select message from Priya", not just "Select".
  • Focus shows a 2px --color-button-1000 ring around the circle (code only).
  • The tick in default and selected is cut out of the disc, so its contrast depends on what's behind the control. Use it on a plain BG/1 or BG/2 surface.
